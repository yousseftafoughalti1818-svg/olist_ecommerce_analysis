# Key findings

Period: 2017-01 to 2018-08, delivered orders only.

- **96,211 orders**, 93,104 customers, **13,181,027 BRL** revenue, average basket 137.00 BRL.
- Growth: monthly revenue is **+263%** higher in summer 2018 than in Q1 2017; best month: 2017-11 (Black Friday).
- Catalogue: 8 categories out of 74 make half of the revenue.
- Delivery: 6.8% of orders arrive after the estimated date. Average review falls from **4.29** (on time) to **1.73** (15+ days late); 1-2 star reviews go from 9.2% to 78.2%.
- Loyalty: **97.0%** of customers ordered only once.
- Sellers: the top 10% of sellers make **67.0%** of revenue.
- Geography: SP, RJ, MG make 63.4% of revenue.
- Payment: 77.0% of orders are paid by credit card.
